Privacy starts locally

Your companion.
Your provider. Your choices.

Persona files, settings, conversations, memory, calendar, and todos stay on the device by default. Provider credentials use Android Keystore-backed storage.

  • ✓ Bring your own OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, or compatible endpoint
  • ✓ Enable only the Tools and Skills you trust
  • ✓ Location is requested only when a location Tool runs
  • ✓ Review the implementation—the entire app is open source

Like any cloud AI app, content needed to answer a request is sent to your configured provider. Mochi makes that boundary explicit instead of hiding it.

Bring Mochi home

Three steps to a more useful phone.

No account with Mochi. No subscription. You choose the AI service that powers it.

  1. 1
    Install

    Download v1.0.6. Choose arm64-v8a for most phones, armeabi-v7a for older 32-bit ARM devices, or universal if you are unsure.

  2. 2
    Connect

    Add your AI provider endpoint, model, and API key in Settings.

  3. 3
    Talk

    Enable the Tools you trust, then type, tap the microphone, or say “Hi Mochi.”
