A voice that works for you.

Start with Android speech. Cloud speech is optional and separate from your model connection.

AndroidiFlytekAzureWake phrase

Android speech · No extra key

  1. Open Settings → Speech and wake and use the system speech connection.
  2. Grant microphone permission and make sure your phone has a speech recognition service and a text-to-speech engine.
  3. Choose an installed voice and try speaking in Companion. No speech account or extension APK is required.

System speech availability and offline support depend on your device and installed services.

Prefer a cloud service?

Open Settings → Speech and wake → Add speech connection. Saving adds an account without selecting it; tap Use when ready.

iFlytek · AppID, APIKey and APISecret
  1. Register at iFlytek Console and enable Real-time Voice Dictation for an application.
  2. Choose iFlytek in Mochi. Copy that application's AppID, APIKey and APISecret into the matching fields and save.
  3. Tap Use, then test recognition with a short phrase. For cloud replies, enable compatible speech synthesis for the same credentials and follow the voice steps below.
Azure Speech · Resource endpoint and key
  1. Create an Azure Speech resource.
  2. Copy its resource endpoint and key. In Mochi choose Azure Speech and fill in Azure Speech endpoint and Azure Speech key. The endpoint resembles https://your-resource.cognitiveservices.azure.com; use the value from your own resource.
  3. Save, tap Use and try a short phrase. This is a Speech resource, not your Azure OpenAI model connection.

Choose how replies sound

  1. Edit a saved cloud connection and enable Also use this provider for speech synthesis if you want cloud replies. It is off by default.
  2. Save, then edit again to load available voices and preview one. The credentials must already be saved; previewing does not switch your active account.
  3. Choose a voice, save and select Use for that connection.

Cloud recognition sends audio to the selected service; cloud synthesis sends reply text and may consume quota. Wake acknowledgements still use Android speech. Switching speech accounts stops the current voice interaction and preview, not text or background tasks.

“Hi Mochi” or “老莫老莫”

Choose the wake phrase in Settings → Speech and wake. System language selects the default: Chinese uses “老莫老莫”; other languages use “Hi Mochi”. Only one phrase is active at a time.

Keep Companion visible, in the foreground, with the screen unlocked. Chat, other pages, lock screen and background use do not listen for wake words. In Chat, hold the input to dictate and tap Send yourself.

Quick checks

No recognition or no sound
  • Check microphone permission, media volume, selected speech account and installed system voice services.
  • For cloud speech, check network access and the resource's recognition/synthesis permissions.
  • Configuration check does not record audio. Test the microphone and preview a voice separately; Chat's reply-mute control can silence replies.
Voice list is unavailable

Save credentials first, then reopen Edit. Cloud synthesis must be enabled to show its voice picker. Replacing credentials requires saving again before loading or previewing voices.
